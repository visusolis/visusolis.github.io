Breathe

→ github.com/snmkl/breathe

a macOS menu bar application that displays an animated breathing circle to guide mindful breathing using the 4-7-8 pattern. It’s really just here so that when you look up you remember to breathe, also was curious how doable it was to make an animation in the menu-bar.
