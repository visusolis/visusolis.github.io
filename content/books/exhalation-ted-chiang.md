Ted Chiang is an Author I've heard a lot of and I wanted to dive into his stories slowly. I appreciated a lot of the smaller ones of exhalation, the longer ones (the digient story for instance) felt a bit like a drag. I think this is more telling of what I enjoy reading, might give some of his other works a try.
