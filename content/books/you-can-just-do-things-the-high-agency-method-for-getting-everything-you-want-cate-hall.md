I got out of the self-help hole a while back but decided to fall back in for once because I sometimes read her newsletter. 

This was not it for me. Though her story and past is interesting, it feels formulaic and ultimately kinda boring. I would have loved to learn more about her process of beating addiction and the struggles of reinventing herself.
