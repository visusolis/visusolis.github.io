I listened to the audiobook, it gripped me end to end.

I experience Ta-Nehisi Coates' letter to his son turned as some sort of sermon. His powerful recollection, experience, history, and feelings became words I surrendered to. 

Hearing the author made it very special, and emotional. Getting an autobiographical view of the black experience in the US taught me more than any history class dared to touch from the other side of the Atlantic.

A book that will stay with me.
