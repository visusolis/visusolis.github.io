[tend](https://github.com/snmkl/tend)
menu bar time tracker. entries save to Apple Calendar — no dock icon, no accounts, no cloud. per-project hourly rates, 30-min billing rounding, CSV export.

[breathe](https://github.com/snmkl/breathe)
menu bar breathing guide. animated circle, 4-7-8 pattern. optional chime every 15 minutes so you remember to look up.

[ricoh.app](https://github.com/snmkl/ricoh.app)
browse and download photos from a Ricoh GR IIIx over Wi-Fi. talks directly to the camera's HTTP server — no phone app, no proxy, no dependencies.

[crosspoint-clippings-sync](https://github.com/snmkl/crosspoint-clippings-sync)
converts Obsidian markdown clippings to EPUB and syncs them to a CrossPoint e-reader over the LAN. only processes changed files, silent if the device is offline.
