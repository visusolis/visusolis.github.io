Reading through [Kening Zhu's journal](https://keningzhu.com/journal) was a flash in the mirror moment.

Seeing someone confidently develop their own space on the internet. Finding myself curious and passionate about exploring all pages, taking in ideas and inspiration. I hadn't experienced that on the internet in a while.

At one point I probably had 25 tabs opened trying to take it all in, but also getting excited to not, not wanting to spoil it all, and just browsing at my own pace.

This felt like a great moment, I want to build this house in 2025. 

I want to find a space where my work, art, thoughts and journey can live. 

This is a space I would want to talk about art, social issues, my growth through the world, the work and the people I meet.

This feels right.
