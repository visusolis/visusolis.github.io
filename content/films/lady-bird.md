I don't get the appeal, and that's because I am not born and raised in the USA, I think.

I enjoyed the pacing and the writing throughout, it was a pleasant watch, but I felt a thick distance to the characters and the story.

Only a few moments gripped me and made the experience worthwhile, but otherwise I guess it just ain't for me.
